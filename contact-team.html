    Our Story

    NextStep was born out of a simple frustration: civic life in Boca Raton was happening everywhere — town halls, volunteer days, school board meetings, park cleanups — but there was no single place to find it, follow it, or feel like you were part of it.

    We're a team of students and local advocates who saw that gap and decided to build the platform we wished existed. What started as a side project turned into a full civic engagement suite — event maps, a badge system, an AI guide, and a community blog — all focused on making participation feel rewarding, not intimidating.

    Our goal isn't to replace traditional civic institutions. It's to make them more accessible, especially for young residents and newcomers who don't yet know where to start. Every feature we build asks one question: does this make it easier for a person to show up?
