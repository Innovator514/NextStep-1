    After You Send

    How your message moves through NextStep

    1

    We read the details

    Your message is reviewed and grouped by topic so the right team member can respond.

    2

    We verify what matters

    For event submissions, we check date, location, audience, link, and whether the opportunity is civic-focused.

    3

    We follow up

    If we need more context, we will reply by email. Most messages get a response within 24-48 hours.

    Sending an event?

    Include the event name, date, time, location, organizer, registration link, category, and a short description. If there is a deadline or age requirement, include that too.

    Looking to collaborate?

    Tell us who you serve, what kind of civic opportunity you are building, and what support you are looking for from NextStep.
